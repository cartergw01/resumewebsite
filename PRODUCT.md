# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyone who wants to understand Carter Wang quickly, weighted equally: founders (including those applying to 886 Studios / ikigai Launchpad), investors and venture recruiters, collaborators, readers, and friends. They arrive from social links, a Substack essay, or a direct share, usually on a phone, and want a fast, truthful read on who Carter is and what he does.

## Product Purpose

carterkowang.com is Carter's personal site: a memorable first impression, then an easy path into topic pages for Work, Writing, and Projects. Success is a visitor leaving with a clear, specific picture of Carter, not a generic resume.

## Positioning

A personal site built as an environment rather than a template: Carter's name in a galaxy, with each topic living on its own floating island (Work is a Taipei island). Only Carter's own words and real facts appear.
