# Taipei rooftop homepage preview

Branch: `codex/taipei-rooftop-home`.

The homepage connects writing, projects, and work in one panoramic rooftop scene. Desktop links illuminate the corresponding scene area on hover or keyboard focus. Mobile shows the panorama above ordinary links with their previews visible. The existing rocket cursor and topic pages remain shared with the original site.

Artwork: `public/taipei-rooftop-v1.png`. Generated with the built-in image generation tool; delivered through Next.js responsive image optimization.

## Artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: full-bleed panoramic background for a refined personal portfolio website.
Create a single coherent Taipei rooftop floating quietly above the curved Earth at night. Cinematic, tactile, beautifully art-directed architectural miniature photography, sophisticated and inviting, not a game screen. Wide 16:9 landscape composition, high resolution.
Composition: leave the entire upper-left half (left 56%, top 53%) mostly uninterrupted deep midnight blue sky, almost black, with a sparse few tiny stars; this is deliberate negative space for large cream typography added later in HTML. A very subtle luminous galaxy is confined to the distant upper-right edge, never bright behind the left text area.
A single continuous rooftop terrace occupies the lower half, seen at a low oblique elevated angle. In the lower-left foreground (about x24%, y72%) is a warmly lit wooden writing desk, recognizable green-shaded desk lamp, notebook, books and a simple chair. In the center-right mid-ground (about x59%, y70%) is a small beautiful terracotta basketball half-court with restrained painted lines and a single hoop. Both sit on the SAME continuous rooftop, connected by a natural concrete walkway and small patches of plants. At right beyond the railing (about x81%, y43%) a compact distant Taipei skyline with recognizable Taipei 101 rises up, many tiny amber-lit windows. Keep all three subjects clearly readable, large enough to recognize, spatially separated by depth rather than separate islands.
The lower terrace edge should have a distinct architectural silhouette that falls away into space. A thin blue atmospheric curve of Earth and softly illuminated clouds are visible below and behind the terrace near the lower right and bottom edge.
Lighting: deep inky/navy shadows, warm amber desk light and windows, soft cool blue Earth rim; emotionally warm and thoughtful. Materials: weathered concrete, warm wood, delicate plants, subtle film texture with crisp focal objects. Realistic perspective, no large fog banks. Rich detail but visually calm.
Constraints: artwork only, absolutely no text, no lettering, no labels, no UI, no logos, no people, no neon cyberpunk signage, no floating disconnected islands, no frames.
